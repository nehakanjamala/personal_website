02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Neha Kanjamala

I'm 20 years old, studying Computer Science at Barrett, The Honors College at Arizona State University. I'm super interested in AI, full-stack software engineering, and product strategy.

People are a huge part of my life, and they're at the center of everything I build. I start with who it's for and how I can make their life a little easier, and I'm always focused on finding the solution that actually works.

What I've been building.

ECMO Bridge

That's why I helped build ECMO Bridge, alongside physicians at Mayo Clinic and a talented team of student developers. Right now, critical hours are lost to manually cold-calling hospitals to find an available ECMO machine for a patient. We're fixing that with a centralized platform every physician can use.

Women in Computer Science

I'm also president of Women in Computer Science, ASU's most active engineering club, hosting four events a week. Founded in 2003, WiCS has been a staple of the ASU tech community. We give underrepresented students in tech a place to grow, through professional development, our mentorship program, and a community built during socials.

Wells Fargo

This summer was easily one of my favorites. I was a software engineering intern at Wells Fargo, where I met some awesome people and built a new LLM-powered credit card recommendation tool from scratch, designed for a platform with 70M+ customers. I also got to travel a ton (shoutout to Wells for sending us to Disney World for orientation week)!!
